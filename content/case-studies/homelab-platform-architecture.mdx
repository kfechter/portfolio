## Introduction

My foray into self hosting my applications began with a single computer purchased at a university surplus sale.

A simple Dell Precision T3500 running Ubuntu and eventually Proxmox led to much more.  Soon my lab consisted of
that Precision, a DL160 G6 rackmount server purchased off of a friend, and a dell R310 given away for free.

The noise and power usage of those three machines led me to ditch enterprise and workstation gear and switch to low power, consumer and prosumer gear.

My current lab topology is as follows.
